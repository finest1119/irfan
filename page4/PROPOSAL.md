# A Malaysian explainer Page for Irfan

**Decision for Irfan · 27 November 2026.** Launch one BM Page, provisionally **Selak Sekejap**, with two recognisable series. A viewer who shares a cutaway of a monsoon drain has a reason to come back for the next rain story: what happens when a builder tries to dry three school uniforms under one awning? One issue desk, narrator and neighbourhood can serve both stories.

| Choice | What the audience gets | Operating trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One combined Page | **Dalam Benda** follows the hidden process. **Bengkel Bar** follows a builder through a failed attempt and a fix. The cover names the series. | One follow, one moderation queue and one Page to qualify for monetisation. A viewer may prefer one series. Track each separately. |
| ED-MY and SME-MY as two Pages | A dedicated reference feed for curious viewers and a separate serial for people who follow Bar’s builds. | Clearer format expectations, but both Pages start from zero. Two publishing identities, two eligibility decisions and two queues compete for the same weekly research and render slots. |

Run a six-week combined trial. Compare each format’s median percentage watched, shares per 1,000 reached and follows per 1,000 reached at the same post age (72 hours). Record sample size and compare within similar issue and time slots. Revisit a split if repeat commenters consistently ask for a dedicated series, the two audiences respond differently across several issues, and both feeds can sustain their own weekly output. A single viral Reel is weak evidence about the Page structure.

## Editorial risk

3R in Malaysia means **race, religion and royalty**. Keep party politics, attacks on officials, identity-based claims and palace speculation out of topic selection. A festive traffic notice can support an episode about queues. Direct the camera at the queue. Omit worshippers, victims, private plates and identifiable homes. Moderate slurs, threats, doxxing and accusations in comments. Give a service-status link when viewers need live information.

Act 866, the Online Safety Act 2025, commenced on 1 January 2026. Its risk mitigation, reporting tools and safety-plan duties primarily address licensed providers. MCMC oversees the framework. An ordinary Page publishing a Reel does not thereby acquire a platform’s licensing duties. The editorial risks here include harmful or defamatory allegations, false emergency updates and misleading synthetic footage. Label realistic scenes “Ilustrasi 3D”, turn on Meta’s AI disclosure and apply the correction workflow in the [issue SOP](ISU-SEMASA-SOP.md). [Act 866, especially Part III](https://lom.agc.gov.my/ilims/upload/portal/akta/outputaktap/2867049_BI/Act%20866-Online%20Safety%20Act%202025.pdf) · [Gazetted commencement](https://lom.agc.gov.my/ilims/upload/portal/akta/outputp/3240953/PUB449_2025.pdf)
