# Three launch pilots

Read each BM column top to bottom for the complete narration. Each slash is a change of on-screen caption, silent in the VO. The time ranges are beat targets **(perlu semak)**. Fit the camera and caption cards to the approved recording. Each film is one continuous vertical move. Keep “Ilustrasi 3D” legible on the opening scene.

## 1 · Dalam Benda · Kenapa kolam masih lepaskan air?

**Topic 01 · target 45 s.** A generic neighbourhood detention pond in monsoon season. Frame one looks through its outlet at a terrace roof. The sound of heavy rain gives way to a steady trickle. Show the date/source in the post if using the November warning. The seasonal narration can stand while that seasonal peg remains valid.

| Time | Beat and camera | BM narration and caption cards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | Hook. Water still leaves the outlet. The rain has stopped. | Hujan dah berhenti. / Kenapa kolam ni / masih lepaskan air? |
| 5–12 s | Move up the bank to the terrace. Follow the earlier downpour as a visual rewind. | Musim tengkujuh, cuba tengok / air dari bumbung / satu taman. / Banyak yang turun / serentak, kan? |
| 12–20 s | Stay with the drop. A section reveals the drain and basin. | Air tu lalu / ikut longkang. / Kalau semua sampai / ke sungai serentak, / alirannya boleh naik / dengan cepat. |
| 20–28 s | Rise with the waterline. Keep inlet and outlet visible together. | Dekat kolam takungan ni, / air masuk laju. / Tapi bukaan kat hujung / cuma lepaskan sebahagian / pada satu masa. |
| 28–36 s | Rain tapers. Pond level starts falling. A leaf passes the outlet. | Jadi baki air / berkumpul dulu. / Bila hujan reda, / air yang tersimpan tadi / masih mengalir keluar / sikit demi sikit. |
| 36–45 s | Return to the opening outlet at the lower waterline. Keep the final trickle audible. | Ruang kolam ada hadnya. / Tapi selagi ada ruang, / air dari satu taman / boleh sampai ke hilir / pada waktu yang berlainan. |

**FB caption** · “Hujan dah reda, tapi air masih keluar dari kolam. Masa tengkujuh ni, jom ikut setitik air dari bumbung sampai ke hilir. Ilustrasi 3D. #SelakSekejap #DalamBenda #MusimHujan”

**Page comment** · “Lepas hujan, apa yang korang perasan dulu dekat kolam takungan kawasan korang?”

**Editor’s evidence.** JPS forecast risk on 26 November. The Ministry of Education’s dated monsoon circular supports the seasonal framing. JPS and PUB engineering references independently support temporary storage and gradual release. A model pond illustrates a design. Keep actual local flood footage, dimensions and prevention guarantees out of the sequence. [Kelantan forecast](https://www.bernama.com/radio/news.php?id=2640033) · [School monsoon circular](https://www.moe.gov.my/storage/files/shares/pengumuman/2026/SURAT%20PERINGATAN%20MTL%202026_2027.pdf) · [JPS manual, quantity control](https://www.water.gov.my/jps/resources/auto%20download%20images/58464682855ea.pdf) · [PUB detention guide](https://www.pub.gov.sg/-/media/PUB/PDF/detentionTank.pdf)

## 2 · Dalam Benda · Bakul siapa sebenarnya?

**Topic 09 · target 46 s.** A receipt curls across a wet-market counter into a partitioned model shopping basket. Use a date strap “Oktober 2026 berbanding Oktober 2025”. For the first spoken number, display the single caption “1.9%”. The partitions represent spending shares. Keep them illustrative and omit invented official weights.

| Time | Beat and camera | BM narration and caption cards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Hook. The receipt and national figure fit in the same frame. | Inflasi Oktober / satu perpuluhan sembilan peratus. / Kenapa belanja dapur / korang rasa lain? |
| 6–13 s | Slide under the basket handle to the groceries. | Cuba tengok bakul ni. / Ada ikan, ada sayur. / Tapi dalam kiraan negara, / bakul tu lagi besar. |
| 13–21 s | Open the basket’s side. Reveal a house key, a bus ticket and a receipt. | Sewa rumah pun masuk. / Tambang pun masuk. / Setiap bahagian ikut / corak belanja isi rumah / secara purata. |
| 21–29 s | The floor area of each compartment represents its share of spending. | Kalau bahagian tu besar, / perubahan harganya pun / lebih terasa dalam kiraan. / Sekarang tengok resit / korang pula. |
| 29–38 s | Follow two receipts across the same stall. Different quantities, same shelf prices. | Katakan minggu ni / korang banyak beli ikan. / Jiran pula banyak / masak telur. / Harga ikan berubah, / kesannya lain. |
| 38–46 s | Return to the original receipt. Keep the year-on-year date strap in view. | Jadi angka Oktober tu / cerita pasal bakul purata / berbanding setahun dulu. / Resit korang cerita / pasal dapur korang. |

**FB caption** · “Inflasi Malaysia pada Oktober 2026 ialah 1.9% berbanding Oktober 2025. Tapi isi bakul korang dengan isi bakul jiran boleh jauh beza. Jom tengok macam mana kiraan tu berfungsi. #DalamBenda #HargaBarang #SelakSekejap”

**Page comment** · “Barang apa yang paling korang perasan bila harga dia berubah dekat kedai?”

**Editor’s evidence.** DOSM is the issuer of the October figure released on 19 November. Its dashboard lets the editor check the month and comparison period. Bank Negara explains the basket weights and why household experiences vary. The fish/egg example is hypothetical. It makes no claim about October fish or egg prices. [DOSM release](https://storage.dosm.gov.my/cpi/cpi_2026-10.pdf) · [OpenDOSM prices](https://open.dosm.gov.my/dashboard/consumer-prices) · [Bank Negara on the basket](https://www.bnm.gov.my/monetary-stability) · [Bank Negara on households](https://www.bnm.gov.my/insights/inflation/ar2025b1)

## 3 · Bengkel Bar · Rak baju musim hujan

**Topic 04 · target 47 s.** A school uniform hangs under Bar’s terrace awning. Bar works silently. Keep the camera inside the porch as he adjusts a model drying rack. For the comparison, use two matching damp sleeves, equal starting moisture, same shade and breeze, and equal elapsed time. Use a visible time-lapse cue “Selepas beberapa ketika”. This illustrates the effect. It promises no specific drying time.

| Time | Beat and camera | BM narration and caption cards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Hook. Bar presses the dry cuff, then the damp underarm seam. | Musim hujan, lengan / baju sekolah dah kering. / Tapi kenapa ketiak / masih lembap? |
| 6–14 s | First fix. Bar slides the rack under shelter. The camera slips between the hangers. | Kat rumah korang / pernah jadi macam ni? / Bar tarik rak / masuk bawah bumbung. / Elok, tempias dah / tak kena baju. |
| 14–21 s | Snag. Sleeves overlap in the narrower space. Reveal the film of moisture. | Tapi ruang sempit. / Lengan baju bertindih. / Bila air kat kain / mula menyejat, / udara celah baju / jadi lembap. |
| 21–29 s | Second fix. Bar adds a staggered rail and spaces hangers. | Jadi Bar tambah / satu palang kat belakang. / Dia susun penyangkut / berselang-seli. / Sekarang lengan baju / dah tak melekap. |
| 29–38 s | Test. Travel with a thread tied to the rack into the new gap. | Nampak benang tu bergerak? / Ada angin lalu / celah kain. / Udara lembap tadi / berganti dengan udara / yang lebih kering. |
| 38–47 s | Payoff. Compare the exposed and overlapped sleeves after the same interval. | Jadi air kat kain / lebih mudah menyejat. / Kalau udara sekeliling / pun lembap, / lambat jugalah. / Patutlah baju kat tengah / selalu tinggal akhir sekali. |

**FB caption** · “Dah alih rak bawah bumbung, baju sekolah masih lembap lagi? Bar cuba ubah jarak penyangkut. Tengok benang kecil kat celah lengan tu. #BengkelBar #JemurBaju #MusimHujan”

**Page comment** · “Bila terpaksa jemur bawah bumbung, korang susun tuala dengan baju macam mana?”

**Editor’s evidence.** The school circular explicitly covers the 2026/27 monsoon. JPS’s separate November forecast supplies contemporary context. Rain guides the setting. The script makes no allegation about a real school or family. Independent physical references support the roles of airflow, exposed wet cloth and ambient humidity. In close-up, use offset arrows or thread for moving air. Avoid visible “steam” around room-temperature uniforms.
