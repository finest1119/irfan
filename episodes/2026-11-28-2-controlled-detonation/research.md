## General explainer (not event claims)
- Lead azide is a primary explosive: small amounts in detonators / blasting caps set off larger charges (PBS above; general chemistry). Larger crystals are more sensitive to friction and shock (PBS; CBS).
- When explosives are too unstable to transport, disposal teams usually destroy them in place ("blow in place") under controlled conditions: standard explosive-ordnance-disposal practice. Worded "usually".
- Public advice (found old ammunition, shells, fireworks: don't touch, don't move, call the authorities): the US Army's "3Rs" explosives safety message, Recognize, Retreat, Report: https://3rs.mil/
