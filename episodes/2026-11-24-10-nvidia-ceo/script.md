### one_expectations
VO: One: a share price is a bet on the future. Investors are usually not paying for last year's profits. They are paying for what they expect next. Change the person steering, and those expectations can change overnight.
Visual: Whip to a road running out of the campus toward the horizon, lined with glowing milestone posts. The posts far ahead slide nearer or farther as the path bends. 2D overlay "1 · EXPECTATIONS" with a simple line chart drawing forward into a dotted future.

### two_uncertainty
VO: Two: markets dislike uncertainty. An empty top job leaves open questions. Who decides? Does the strategy change? Often, investors demand a lower price until they get answers.
Visual: Whip through a window into a bright corner office: an empty chair behind a clean desk, soft fog rolling over the floor. 2D overlay "2 · UNCERTAINTY" with question-mark chips popping around the chair.

### three_board
VO: Three: the board. Shareholders elect directors, and the directors hire and can remove the chief executive. So a board vote is usually the moment a leadership question gets a clear answer.
Visual: Whip down the corridor into a glass boardroom: a long table with a ring of empty chairs, a small white light switching on at each seat in turn. 2D overlay "3 · THE BOARD" with a flow strip: shareholders → directors → chief executive.

### metaphor

Visual: Whip out the boardroom window to the campus lake, where a large toy-like ship glides past. Camera rises to its empty bridge and wheel; fog lifts as the wheel steadies.

### takeaway
