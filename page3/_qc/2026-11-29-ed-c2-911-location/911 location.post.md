# You Call 911 and Can't Speak. Here's How They Find You

**Keyword / filename:** 911 location

## Facebook caption

When you call 911 from a cell phone, the call reaches a nearby cell tower and the network sends it to your local 911 center, along with which tower and which side of it picked up the call: a rough area. Many newer phones also send their own location automatically, built from GPS, Wi-Fi and other signals, which can often put you much closer on the dispatcher's map (it depends on your phone, carrier and 911 center). In many areas you can also text 911: give your address in the text, and remember the rule, call if you can, text if you can't. Did you know you could text 911?
