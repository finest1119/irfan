# The Bananas at Your Store Were Picked Green, Then Ripened on Purpose

## Facebook caption

Those yellow bananas started out hard and green. Growers cut them early so they survive the long trip, and they ride in cool containers, around 55 degrees, so they don't ripen on the way. Near your city they go into sealed ripening rooms, where a little ethylene gas is added. Ethylene is a natural plant hormone that bananas make themselves; the room just starts a whole load at once. A few days later they turn yellow together and head to the shelf. Green, yellow, or spotty: how do you like yours?
