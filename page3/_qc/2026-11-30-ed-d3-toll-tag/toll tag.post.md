# You Drive Through a Toll at Highway Speed and Never Stop

**Keyword / filename:** toll tag

## Facebook caption

No booth, no braking, and the toll still gets paid. The little tag on your windshield is a radio ID. As you pass, a reader on the overhead gantry pings it, the tag answers with its ID, and the toll is matched to your account and charged automatically. Many sticker tags don't even have a battery: the reader's radio signal powers them for that instant. No tag? Cameras snap your license plate and the bill comes by mail. Tag on the windshield or bill by mail: which are you?
