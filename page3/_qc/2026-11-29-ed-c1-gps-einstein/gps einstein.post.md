# Your Phone's GPS Would Be Off by Miles a Day Without Einstein

**Keyword / filename:** gps einstein

## Facebook caption

The blue dot on your map depends on clocks more than twelve thousand miles up. Your phone times signals from at least four GPS satellites to work out where you are, but up there weaker gravity makes clocks tick faster, and even after their speed slows them a bit they gain about 38 millionths of a second a day. Signals move at light speed, so left uncorrected that tiny gap would add up to miles of error in a single day. That's why the satellite clocks are tuned to tick slightly slow before launch. Blue dot or street signs: which do you trust?
