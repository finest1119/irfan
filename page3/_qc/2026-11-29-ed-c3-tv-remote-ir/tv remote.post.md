# Your TV Remote Flashes a Light You Can't See, but Your Phone Camera Can

**Keyword / filename:** tv remote

## Facebook caption

Every time you press a button, your TV remote flashes an infrared LED, a light just past red that your eyes can't see. Each button sends its own pattern of super-fast flashes, and a small sensor on the TV reads it. Many phone cameras can pick up that light, so it shows up on screen as a purple-white flash. Point the remote at your phone's camera and press a button; if nothing shows, try the selfie camera. Did you see the flash?
