## Voiceover script

In most tall buildings, the elevator motor isn't lifting your whole car. A hidden weight is helping.
Your car hangs from steel ropes that loop over a grooved wheel at the top and down to a heavy counterweight.
That counterweight matches the empty car plus about forty to fifty percent of a full load.
So the motor mostly moves the difference, like a seesaw with a friend on the other end.
As your car rises, the counterweight drops, and they pass each other in the shaft.
And if a car ever goes too fast, safety brakes clamp onto the rails and stop it.
