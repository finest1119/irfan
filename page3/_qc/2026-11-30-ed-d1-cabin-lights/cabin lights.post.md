# Why the Cabin Lights Dim When Your Plane Lands

**Keyword / filename:** cabin lights

## Facebook caption

Right before landing, the cabin lights go down, and it isn't to help you nap. Your eyes need minutes to adjust to the dark, so dimming early gives them a head start. If everyone ever had to leave in a hurry, the floor lights and exit signs would already stand out. Many airlines also ask for window shades up so the crew can see outside and choose a safe exit. And to be certified, big airliners must show everyone can get out in 90 seconds, in the dark, using only half the exits. Window seat or aisle seat: which do you pick?
