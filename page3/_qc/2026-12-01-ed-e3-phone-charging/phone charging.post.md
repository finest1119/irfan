# Why Your Phone Charges Slower After About 80 Percent

**Keyword / filename:** phone charging

## Facebook caption

Your phone races to about 80 percent, then crawls the rest of the way, and that's on purpose. At first the charger pushes a strong, steady current. Near full, the battery reaches its voltage limit, so the charger holds it there and lets the current taper off. Some phones also learn your routine, hold around 80 percent overnight, and finish just before you wake up, because sitting at a full charge for hours wears a battery faster. Do you charge your phone overnight, or top it up during the day?

## Sources

- Google Pixel Help, Get the most life from your Pixel phone battery (Adaptive Charging learns your habits and completes charging right before you typically unplug, so the phone isn't at 100% for a long time; Limit to 80% option): https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/6090612
