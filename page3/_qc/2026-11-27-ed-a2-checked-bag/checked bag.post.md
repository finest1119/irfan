## Voiceover script

You drop your bag at the counter. It disappears. Here's the ride it takes under the airport.
First, the tag. It carries a ten-digit number, printed as a barcode.
On the belt, lasers and cameras read it from every side, because the bag can land any way up. Some airlines add a radio chip, too.
Then it's scanned for explosives, often while it moves, before it ever reaches the plane.
The computer knows your flight, so paddles called diverters push your bag onto the right belt at the right moment.
It ends in your flight's pile, then rides a cart to the plane's belly.
