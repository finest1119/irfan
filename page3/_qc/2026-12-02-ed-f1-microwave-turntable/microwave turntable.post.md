## Voiceover script

Why can one spoonful of microwaved soup be piping hot when the next is barely warm?
Microwaves bounce off the metal walls and overlap, so some spots get more energy than others.
Water molecules in your soup absorb that energy. Where they absorb more, the soup can heat up faster.
The glass tray turns to carry different parts of your soup through stronger and weaker spots.
But watch the very center of that spinning tray. It stays in the same spot the whole time.
Stirring halfway through helps even out the heat. Which leftovers do you always stop to stir? Tell us below.
