# BUILD-SPEC — Bolts, Torque & Mercer Hollow (for the Blender builder)

Inputs: `page2/character.json`, `page2/world_state.json` (generated by `page2/scripts/gen_world_state.py`;
edit the script and re-run instead of hand-editing it), concept art in `page2/concept/` (picks in its README).
Target: Windows laptop, RTX 3050 **4 GB**, Blender 5.3, Cycles + OptiX, persistent data ON, one GPU render at a time.
Scene VRAM limit: **≤ 3 GB** (PROPOSAL 7.3).

## 1. Scenes = locations
- Every **location** (`locations.mercer_hollow`, later `trips.<id>`) and every **interior**
  (`locations.mercer_hollow.interiors.<room>`) is its **own light Blender scene/.blend**, built once per reel.
  The exterior scene never contains interior furniture, and interiors never load the terrain, woods or street.
- **The one-shot camera stays inside a single location per reel.** A reel can open with a whip-out through a door
  or window (interior to exterior, or the reverse): model the room's door/window opening plus a 2D or very
  low-poly backdrop card of the matching exterior view. The whip blur hides the scene boundary. If a true
  continuous pass between scenes is ever needed, render the two segments and join them inside the whip blur
  frames. That is the only allowed hidden join, and `qc_oneshot` must whitelist it.
- Interior coordinates are local to the room (origin = floor centre, world axes). `world_anchor` says where the
  room sits in Mercer Hollow, so window views and backdrop cards line up with the real exterior.
- Interiors get the same budget: ≤ 3 GB, 2K textures only on the hero (Bolts + the current build), 1K for the
  rest, instanced clutter (boxes, cans, tools, joists, rafters), one 2K HDRI or a sun plus area lights.

## 2. Bolts (≤ 30k tris visible, one 2K core set)
> **LOCKED BODY (25 Nov 2026, Irfan-approved, session 11/12):** Bolts is the Quaternius Superhero_Male base
> (`BOLTS_IMPL=s9`) with the **s11 normalized proportions** (`MH_BOLTS_PROP=s11`): 1.778 m, trunk 34–36 cm wide,
> 2 cm belly, upper arm 0.304 m / forearm 0.259 m, wrist at crotch level in idle, 18.9 cm hands. Both are the code
> defaults. Full numbers: `character.json` → `bolts.proportions.locked_body`. **Do not change the body mid-series**;
> never mix s6 / the s9 profile into an episode. The tri budget below is exceeded (electrician + harness ≈ 34.7k
> visible), accepted because it is only ~5 MB of geometry.

- One armature. Core mesh = body, head, hair/beard cards and the 4 signatures, on a single 2K atlas. Each outfit
  is a separate low mesh (≤ ~9k tris including props) with its own **1K** set, parented to the same armature.
  Swap outfits with keyframed `hide_render` + `hide_viewport`. Hidden objects don't go to the GPU. Swaps happen
  only inside a whip (PROPOSAL 4.2-6).
- Hide the body faces under each outfit with a mask modifier driven by vertex groups (`hide_under_<outfit>`).
  This avoids clipping and saves tris.
- Signatures are separate objects child-of'd to bones, so no outfit can lose them. Check every outfit in a
  front, side and back turntable: **glasses on the forehead below any brim/cuff, red bandana, pencil behind
  the RIGHT ear, tape measure at the LEFT front hip.**
- Hair: scalp shell plus a short card set. When headgear is on, hide the top cards (`hat_root` bone).
- Clips: 10 actions in `character.json` (30 fps) as NLA actions; hand props parent to `prop_R`/`prop_L`.
- Simple-realistic look: SSS off (or a cheap Principled setting with low subsurface), roughness-driven skin,
  no displacement.

## 3. Torque (≤ 5k tris, one 1K set)
- Feathers come from the normal map, with a few alpha cards only at the wing tips and tail. 6 clips; `waddle`
  and `chase` loop in place, and a path drives root motion.

## 4. Mercer Hollow exterior
- **Terrain**: one grid mesh around 200 × 200 m at about 1–2 m resolution near the lot, decimated to 8–10 m out
  to 300 m. Heights come from `terrain.height_grid` (10 m samples), refined with the analytic profile in the
  generator: creek channel 3 m wide, a 2.0 m waterfall at y≈35.5, and the pond. Texture: a 1K tiling grass +
  dirt + rock mix by slope, plus one 1K mask. **Cut-away slices** (`section` camera) are a boolean of the
  terrain with a soil-layer material, enabled only in the reels that use one.
- **Instanced, never duplicated**: trees (3 species × 2 LODs; woods ≈ 1 tree/40 m²), fence posts and boards,
  split-rail sections, utility poles, sidewalk slabs, grass clumps (only within about 25 m of the current
  waypoint), rocks, neighbour houses (2 models, `nbr_A`/`nbr_B`, colour via an object-info attribute).
- **Hero (2K)**: house, Bolts, the current build. Everything else is 1K or smaller. Far hills, town and water
  tower are 2D cards or ≤ 500-tri meshes with baked textures. HDRI 2K, `late_summer` by default; `dusk` and
  `frost`/`snow` presets come later.
- **Builds**: `builds[]` is empty until episodes are approved. Each build has 3 LODs (hero / lod1 > 60 m /
  lod2 > 150 m), swapped per camera waypoint and never mid-frame in view, to avoid popping.
- **Budget check**: after building, render the `aerial_overview` and `back_yard` spots and log peak VRAM
  (GPU-Z or `nvidia-smi`). That figure must be ≤ 3 GB with Bolts, Torque, the pickup and one build present.

## 5. Layout decisions I made (please confirm)

40 m away. That contradicts section 2.1 ("behind it the lot drops to a creek and climbs a wooded ridge",
ridge in the east). I followed the prose. The order runs **west to east: street/cul-de-sac → house (front faces
west) → fenced back yard → drop to the creek → meadow → ridge**. To flip it, edit the constants in the
generator.

## 6. Ambiguities and open questions
1. **Creek side** (see above). This is the biggest decision, and everything else hangs on it.
2. **Lot size**: 400 × 520 ft is **4.8 acres**, not "about 3". I used 400 ft east–west and 520 ft north–south,
   on a pie-shaped cul-de-sac lot.
3. **Slope**: with the ridge at +80 ft inside a 400 ft-wide lot, the meadow averages **22–30°, max ~37°**. That
   is steep, but it works for the Build #10 funicular. Options: accept it, or let the ridge sit off-lot further
   east.
4. **Ram pump (#3)**: the 6.5 ft drive fall is placed at the waterfall (bottom z 0.4). The ridge pad is **~79 ft**
   above it, not the proposal's 65 ft. The `ram_tank_alt_site` (z 20.2) gives exactly 65 ft, but it is not
   "next to the turbine". Fact-check the ratio at script time.
5. **Creek crossing**: the creek sits between the house and the ridge, and the proposal names no bridge. I added
   a `creek_crossing` plank footbridge, flagged `proposed`.
6. **Neighbour gag**: `nb_north`'s side yard shares our north privacy fence (y≈22–23). This is invented, to make
   "the neighbour peeking over the fence" possible.
7. **Budget split**: I assumed the 30k tris include the active outfit, and that outfits use their own 1K sets
   rather than the single 2K set. The proposal is unclear on both.
8. **Tape-measure hip** and **pencil colour** weren't specified. I chose the left front hip and yellow-orange
   #F2A900, so the pencil reads against gray hair and doesn't compete with the red bandana.
9. **Clip counts**: the proposal lists ~8 Bolts and 4 Torque clips; this spec uses the task's 10 and 6.
10. **Schema drift**: the proposal sketch uses `day/idle/night/posted`. The build schema here uses
    `build_no/idle_anim/posted_date` and adds `location` and `interior`. `night` (beacon) and `cables_pipes`
    have no field in the task schema. `cables_pipes` is kept as a separate top-level list; add a `night_anim`
    field if dusk reels need it.
11. **Trips vs the US-first rule**: trip locations like a "hometown/kampung" or "remote work abroad" conflict with
    PROPOSAL section 0 ("no locally-specific references outside the US"). The trip schema is a placeholder;
    Irfan should confirm whether trips must be US-only (a lake cabin, the old family farm in another state) or
    are an exception.
12. **Interiors**: the proposal never mentions a basement or attic. Room sizes are invented to fit the house
    footprint: the basement and attic are full-footprint, and the kitchen and living room sit side by side on
    the ground floor.

## Decisions on the open questions (planning session, 24 Nov 2026)
1. **Creek position:** between the house and the ridge (east of the house), as `world_state.json` has it. The proposal sketch was only schematic.
2. **Lot size:** keep 400 × 520 ft; the proposal now says "about 4–5 acres".
3. **Slope:** too steep for the look. Stretch the ridge farther east and/or lower it so the meadow averages **≤ 15–18°**. The funicular track (Build #10) may be steeper (up to ~30°) on its own cut.
4. **Heights:** the ridge-pad tank must sit **~65 ft above the ram pump** (Build #3 script). Ridge top ≈ 70–80 ft above the house floor; the pump sits low at the creek bottom. Adjust terrain to match.
5. **Footbridge** over the creek: approved (needed for walking shots).
6. **Trips / hometown:** resolved — PROPOSAL section 3.3 (US-first trips + an occasional remote-work trip abroad).
7. **Concept picks:** `bolts_turnaround_v4` (the builder fixes the beard to a full short salt-and-pepper beard, uses round safety glasses on the forehead, and moves the tape to the left hip), `bolts_outfits_lineup_v1` (good reference: signatures visible in every outfit), `torque_goose_sheet_v1`, `mercer_hollow_aerial_v1`.
